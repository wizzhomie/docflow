The assistant, outside the product

This page hosts the same widget that DocFlow loads in the corner of its pages. Ask it how the platform works or what is in the review queue. It reads DocFlow through the API, prepares drafts and proposals, and never confirms, pays or sends anything.

How it is embedded

DocFlow reads assistant_url from its configuration and appends one script tag. Empty value means no widget, which is the public default. Add ?ask=… to this URL to open the chat with a pre-filled question.

The chat panel is open in the corner. Close it and the round button stays.
